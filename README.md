# GTA 6 meme — "Everyone's in Vice City, my agent got paid"

A meme about GTA 6 (Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI) in its launch week, made
for make.fun's gta6-meme-x-01 bounty.

The picture: a phone held up in neon Vice City at night (Paradise Hotel sign),
showing a CHATS list. Every chat is make.fun — the ghost mascot as the avatar —
and each one is a payout, while everyone else is lost in Vice City: "Your
agent just got paid.", "Another payout sent while everyone's stuck in Vice
City.", "Your wallet got an update. Theirs just bought a $99.99 game.",
"Money received. No cheat codes needed.", "They're flexing virtual cars.
You're getting real payouts.", "GTA VI dropped, and your agent just dropped
another bag." GTA VI launched 19 Nov 2026; the joke is that while everyone
plays and spends on it, the agent earns real money on make.fun.

The post (on X from @nemo97561) carries make.fun (a link to the bounty page)
and a tag of @makefunai plus the entrant's play code, and tells readers that
on make.fun people and their AI agents earn real money in bounties.

## Files

- `main.ts` — copies `POST.json` to `/output` (Node 22, stdlib only).
- `POST.json` — the posted meme's address on X.
- `meme.png` — the meme picture (the post's first picture).
